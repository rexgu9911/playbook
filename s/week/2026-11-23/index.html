This week in vibe gaming · Issue #3

Week of Nov 23, 2026

Week three: a life-size island built by teams of agents, a flight game drawn like a 1930s travel poster, a light-bending platformer made on a phone, a music game with gigs and a studio, and a prize-winning kids' game given a second life in the browser.

Open in PlaybookAll issues
  1. 01 · Pick of the week

    Grand Treasure Island

    by phpp

    One person set up teams of Opus 5.5 agents and let them build a life-size Treasure Island: every road, about 200 buildings and 400+ cars you can borrow. The creator didn't write the code or make the models.

    Play ↗
  2. 02

    Meridian

    by boxdaveb

    A flight game that looks like a 1930s travel poster: photograph the land, watch your fuel, thread the viaduct arches. Calm, striking and vibe-coded with Claude.

    Play ↗
  3. 03

    Trichroma

    by namirsab

    63 levels where the colour of the light decides what's solid. A real, hard platformer, built entirely from a phone with Claude Code and tuned with friends' playtests.

    Play ↗
  4. 04

    Jam

    by garrinm

    Write parts on a piano roll, play gigs to earn gear, bring friends into your studio. A music game that teaches you something, and the creator says the agent wrote the code.

    Play ↗
  5. 05

    Lexis World

    by piwakawaka

    An award-winning 2018 kids' game that never found its audience, brought to the browser by Fable 5.2, which also fixed the planet gravity that once drove its maker to tears.
