This week in vibe gaming · Issue #2

Week of Nov 16, 2026

Week two: a photoreal Manhattan you can drive and fly through, a logistics sim written by arguing agents, a body-cam shooter in plain three.js, a web crawler's own platformer, and a tennis game that's more than it looks.

Open in PlaybookAll issues
  1. 01 · Pick of the week

    CROSSTOWN

    by @mattshumer_

    An early look at a photoreal Manhattan you can drive, fly a helicopter over or walk through, right in Chrome. Still rough, but it already feels bigger than most browser games.

    Play ↗
  2. 02

    Dread & District

    by @emollick

    A fantasy logistics sim where good routing beats evil, made by a team of agents arguing about art, lore and jokes. A rare look at what agents build on their own.

    Play ↗
  3. 03

    ELITE WARFARE

    by jmbl1685

    A body-cam tactical shooter with bots, peer-to-peer multiplayer and two maps, built in plain three.js with Claude Code. You don't need an engine for this.

    Play ↗
  4. 04

    CCBot: The Great Crawl

    by ccgreg

    The Common Crawl team made a pixel-art platformer starring their own web crawler, and put Claude in the credits. Charming and a little self-aware.

    Play ↗
  5. 05

    Tennis

    by @photonstorm

    It looks like a Pong clone until you play it. It started as a joke post from the maker of Phaser and got shared everywhere.
