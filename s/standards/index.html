Quality standards

What makes the cut

Playbook is picky on purpose. Every game here passes the same bar, and every weekly pick is chosen by a person.

Open in PlaybookGame index
  1. 01

    Made mostly with AI

    The creator says in public that AI wrote most of the code, and we link to where they said it.

  2. 02

    Free to play in a browser

    No download, no sign-in and no payment to start playing.

  3. 03

    An original game

    No clones of other people's games, characters or names. Inspired by is fine; copies are not.

  4. 04

    A real game

    Something you can play and win, lose or score in, not a tech demo, a tool or a screensaver.

  5. 05

    Not a content farm

    We list a creator's best work, not hundreds of near-identical games.

  6. 06

    Credited and respected

    We link to the creator's own page and show the image they publish. Nothing is copied or hosted here, and creators can claim or remove a listing at any time.

How weekly picks are chosen

Each week a person picks five games that stand out for how they look, how they feel to play, or what they try. Every pick says why.
